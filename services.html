05. Authority

Electronic Press Kit

Your brand, packaged for the world. An Electronic Press Kit (EPK) is your brand's official media document: a curated, professionally designed dossier that tells journalists, editors, event organizers, and collaborators exactly who you are, what you stand for, and why you are worth covering. We build your EPK from scratch, weaving together your brand biography, key achievements, service offerings, high-resolution imagery, press coverage, and contact details into a single, beautifully produced asset ready to open doors. From national media features to speaking engagements, a Rosen Relations EPK positions you as the authority your brand already is.
