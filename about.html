Our Founder

Nani Rosen

Founder & Principal · Rosen Relations

Widely known as The Luxury Lens, a distinction that speaks to both aesthetic mastery and a strategic ability to see brands in their highest, most intentional form.

Chantal "Nani" Rosen is the founder of Rosen Relations, a luxury brand relations and visibility strategy firm operating at the intersection of visual conceptualization, editorial direction, and brand elevation. With a background in modeling, visual storytelling, and creative direction, she brings a refined editorial eye to every engagement, ensuring each brand's presence is curated with precision and purpose.

She has partnered with globally recognized brands including Breitling, Neiman Marcus, Kendra Scott, and America's Heart. As a woman-owned firm, Rosen Relations challenges outdated industry norms and redefines how visibility, influence, and creative leadership are cultivated.

The Company

Rosen Relations

In a world where artificial intelligence can replicate nearly anything, Rosen Relations was built on the one thing it cannot: genuine human taste, lived experience, and the instinct that only comes from being in the room.

Founded in 2020, Rosen Relations is a culture-shaping force designed to position brands with intention, authority, and unmistakable presence in competitive luxury markets. We partner with discerning founders, executives, and brands who understand that how you are seen is inseparable from what you are worth.

Rosen Relations solves visibility problems. Visibility, as Nani describes it, is the stage upon which your entire sales funnel is set. We bridge the gap between a business's true worth and how the world currently perceives it, transforming that distance into a competitive advantage through strategic positioning, authentic narrative, and precision-crafted presence.
